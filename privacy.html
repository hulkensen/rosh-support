ROSH Privacy Policy

Updated: 11 October 2026

ROSH provides prayer and Qibla tools, mosque information and Sadaqah project discovery. This policy covers the existing guest experience and the optional account features introduced in version 1.1 and available on the ROSH website.

Guest use and device preferences

You can browse the main screens without creating an account. Language, country, currency and demonstration reminder preferences are stored on your device. The earlier version 1.0 does not include the account features described below.

Optional accounts and information you submit

If you create an account, we process your email address, an account identifier and any display name you choose. Authentication is handled by the hosted authentication service. These details are used to sign you in, display your profile and associate your submissions with your account.

If you create an organization or fundraiser, we store the information you submit, including its title, description, project location, category, fundraising goal and any beneficiary details you choose to provide. Approved project information is visible to other users. Please do not include private or sensitive information about another person without their permission.

Reports about projects and moderation decisions are stored to review submissions and manage safety. These records are associated with the relevant account or project. Donation history screens show records associated with your account; demonstration receipts do not represent real payments.

Service providers and use of data

ROSH uses a Bolt-hosted Supabase database, authentication and server functions to provide account, fundraiser, moderation and account-deletion features. Information is sent to these services as needed to provide those features. It is not used by ROSH for advertising tracking or sold to data brokers.

Location

Device location, where supported, is optional and used on the device for local prayer and Qibla calculations. ROSH does not upload your precise device coordinates to its account database. Project and organization locations that you type into a submission are stored as part of that submission and may be displayed publicly after approval. Some map and mosque information is demonstration data.

Payments

The current donation experience is a demonstration. ROSH does not process real donations or collect payment-card details in this version. Any future payment functionality will require an updated disclosure before it is activated.

Access, correction and deletion

You can edit your display name in Profile. To delete your account, sign in and choose Profile → Delete my account, then confirm. The server removes the authentication account, unpublishes its fundraisers and anonymizes the profile. Moderation records may be retained to preserve the review history. You can also contact us about access, correction or deletion of your information. Information remains in the service while needed for the account and submitted projects; signing out alone does not delete it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ROSH at romoetechsystem@hotmail.com. Never send us your password.
