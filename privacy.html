ROSH Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

ROSH is designed to provide prayer, Qibla, mosque information and a demonstration Sadaqah experience.

Data collection

ROSH does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. Profile preferences such as name, language and country are stored locally on the device.

Location

Location access is optional. If enabled, the app uses location on the device to personalize local prayer and Qibla information. Precise location is not sent to the developer and is not stored on a remote server.

Payments and accounts

The current version is a demo. It does not process real donations or payments and does not require an account.

Contact

Questions may be sent to romoetechsystem@hotmail.com.
