ROSH: Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your ROSH: Sadaqah & Prayer account and associated data without reinstalling or opening the app.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email romeotechsystem@gmail.com from the email address registered to your ROSH account.
  2. Use the subject “ROSH account deletion” and state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. We may ask you to confirm ownership before processing the request. Never send your password.

To request deletion of particular personal information while keeping your account, use the same address and specify the information you want removed. Information needed to maintain your account may require account deletion instead.

Delete inside the app

Sign in, open Profile → Delete my account, and confirm. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

What happens to your data?

The account-deletion process removes the authentication account, unpublishes its fundraisers and anonymizes the profile. Unpublished project and moderation records may remain to preserve the review history. These retained records do not have an automatic expiry period; contact us to request review and removal of any remaining personal information.

The current app processes no real payments. Demonstration receipts are not real donation transactions.
